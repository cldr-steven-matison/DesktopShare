**There is no official or publicly available “Top 100” ranking** of X (Twitter) accounts that primarily post viral streaming clips from Twitch and Kick. The space consists of:

- A few general clip/highlight accounts (mostly Kick-focused in recent data).
- Hundreds of smaller, often streamer-specific fan accounts (typically 100–5k followers).

X is less dominant for short-form clip virality than TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Instagram Reels, so dedicated high-follower clip accounts are relatively rare. Many viral clips are posted by the streamers themselves, their teams, or scattered fans.

### Additional Notes

- **General vs. Niche**: Most high-engagement accounts are either broad Kick clip pages or hyper-specific fan accounts for one streamer. True “both Twitch + Kick” general accounts are rare and usually small.
- **Other common patterns**: Many accounts exist for big names like Kai Cenat, Adin Ross, HasanAbi, Ludwig, etc., but they typically have lower follower counts (often under 2k) unless the streamer is extremely popular.
- **Official/Related accounts**: @Twitch (official, occasional clips/announcements), @StreamsClips (top Twitch clips via Streams Charts, lower activity/followers).
- **How virality works here**: Success depends more on timing, captioning, and the original streamer’s audience than raw follower count. Many clips go viral from smaller accounts if they catch a big moment.
