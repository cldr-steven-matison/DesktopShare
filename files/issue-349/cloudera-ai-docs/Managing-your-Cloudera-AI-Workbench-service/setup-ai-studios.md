# AI Studios Overview (Technical Preview)

> Canonical URL: https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/

Product: machine-learning 1.5.5

## [AI Studios Overview (Technical Preview)](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-ai-studios-overview.html)

Cloudera AI Studios is a comprehensive suite of low-code tools designed to simplify the development, customization, and deployment of generative AI solutions within enterprises. This suite empowers organizations to operationalize AI workflows quickly and efficiently by leveraging real-time enterprise data. It provides scalable, cost-effective, and trustworthy AI applications, fostering seamless collaboration between business and IT teams.

## [Enabling AI Studios](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-setting-up-enabling-ai-studios.html)

Administrators must enable the AI Studios option to allow users to build and deploy AI-powered applications.

## [Managing RAG Studio](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-managing-rag-studio.html)

Manage the entire lifecycle of LLMs with the help of RAG Studio, empowering you to optimize AI workloads while enhancing the accuracy and efficiency of your models.

### [RAG Studio Overview](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-rag-studio-overview.html)

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Studio is a no-code application for creating RAG chatbots that combine the power of retrieval and generation to provide accurate and efficient responses.

### [Key features for RAG Studio](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-key-features-for-rag-studio.html)

RAG Studio allows you to create an application with a conversational interface (chatbot) that can be used to converse with a connected large language model, and comes with the functionality to enable retrieval-augemented generation to improve the performance and accuracy of the model’s responses.

### [Launching RAG Studio within a project](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-launching-rag-studio-within-a-project.html)

RAG Studio is compatible with Cloudera AI Inference service, AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, and Open AI enabling you to select different LLM and embedding models tailored to your specific needs.

### [Configuring RAG Studio](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-configuring-rag-studio.html)

After launching RAG Studio, an initial configuration is required, with optional settings available for additional customization. Without this configuration, the Studio will not be able to access any AI models.

## [Managing Fine Tuning Studio](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-managing-fine-tuning-studio.html)

Customize and optimize large language models to meet the specific needs of your organization.

### [Fine Tuning Studio Overview](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-fine-tuning-studio-overview.html)

The Fine Tuning Studio is a comprehensive application and ecosystem that enables you to manage the entire lifecycle of Large Language Models (LLMs), including training, fine-tuning, and evaluation. With a streamlined approach to organizing and dispatching Cloudera AI workloads, this AI studio is specifically designed to support tasks related to LLM training and evaluation.

### [Key Features for Fine Tuning Studio](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-key-features-for-fine-tuning-studio.html)

The Fine Tuning Studio is a powerful tool that enables you to customize and optimize large language models to meet the specific needs of your organization.

### [Launching Fine Tuning Studio within a project](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/setup-ai-studios/topics/ml-launching-fine-tuning-studio-within-a-project.html)

You can launch Fine Tuning Studio on the Cloudera AI Platform to manage the life cycle of LLMs from training, and fine-tuning, to evaluating LLMs.
