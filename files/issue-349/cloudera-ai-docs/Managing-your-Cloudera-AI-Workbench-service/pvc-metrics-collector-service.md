# Cloudera AI Metrics Collector Service overview

> Canonical URL: https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/pvc-metrics-collector-service/

Product: machine-learning 1.5.5

## [Cloudera AI Metrics Collector Service overview](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/pvc-metrics-collector-service/topics/ml-metrics-collector-service-overview.html)

The Metrics Collector service shows metrics data for Cloudera AI.

## [Visualization in Cloudera AI quota management](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/pvc-metrics-collector-service/topics/ml-visualization-in-grafana-dashboard-pvc.html)

The Grafana dashboard, called Cloudera AI Quota Management, gives details about user and group resource quota limits.

### [Cloudera AI Quota Management dashboard](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/pvc-metrics-collector-service/topics/ml-quota-management-dashboard-pvc.html)

The Cloudera AI Quota Management Dashboard provides an overview of resource quota allocation and utilization of the users or groups within the Cloudera AI environment. Users can filter the dashboard by selecting the namespace for the Cloudera AI Workbench, choosing the user or group type, and specifying the user or group name.

### [Troubleshooting for Cloudera AI Metrics Collector Service](https://docs.cloudera.com/machine-learning/1.5.5/pvc-metrics-collector-service/topics/ml-pvc-metrics-collector-services-troubleshooting.html)

Restarting the Cloudera AI Metrics Collector Service might lead to multiple entries appearing in the dashboard.
